Privacy policy

Effective 30/9/2026

We collect as little as we can get away with, and we say so here plainly rather than in a document meant to be skimmed past.

What we collect.

  • Your email address, so we can tell you when the kit is real. Nothing else.

We do not collect anything about where you personally are located, and the page sets no tracking cookies.

What we don't do.

We don't sell your data. We don't use it for advertising. We don't share it beyond the email provider that stores the waitlist and any infrastructure provider strictly needed to run the site.

How long we keep it.

We keep your email address for as long as the kit waitlist is running, and for a reasonable period afterward. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Your rights.

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Write to olgazetechnologies@gmail.com and a person will answer.

Changes.

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll post it here and, where we reasonably can, tell you directly.
